    Preface

    These Emergency Medical Dispatch (EMD) Protocols are intended for use by trained and competent professionals as a guide to evidence-based best practices for out-of-hospital emergency care in developing health systems. They are not a substitute for training and should only be used by those specifically trained on the procedures documented within.

    This document is not intended to be a training tool or textbook. This document is reviewed and updated every two years, whilst open access with no copyright restrictions. If used, it is the user’s responsibility to ensure that these protocols are the most up-to-date. Every effort has been made to ensure this document is free from errors. If one is found, please get in touch with the author, who will provide an update once it is released.
